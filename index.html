Two-Wheel Self-Balancing Robot with BLE ControlBLE 遥控双轮自平衡机器人

ELEC 391 · Team of 44 人团队

An inverted-pendulum robot that stays upright on two wheels, drives to commanded positions, turns, and takes commands from a phone over Bluetooth Low Energy.

一台倒立摆式双轮机器人：保持直立、行驶到指定位置、转向，并通过低功耗蓝牙接收手机指令。

  • Three-loop control on an Arduino Nano 33 BLE Sense: a ~190 Hz PID balance loop, a 20 Hz encoder-based PD position loop that sets the target lean angle, and a P-only turning loop.
  • Sensor fusion: complementary filter (K = 0.98) on the onboard BMI270 IMU sampled at 400 Hz, with automatic gyro-bias calibration at start-up; interrupt-driven wheel encoders.
  • Motor drive: two DRV8871 drivers from a 12 V supply, voltage-divided encoder inputs to protect the 3.3 V logic, and per-motor deadband compensation measured on the assembled robot.
  • Non-blocking BLE: custom service polled every few control cycles, with ramped position targets so commands never disturb loop timing.
  • 三环控制：在 Arduino Nano 33 BLE Sense 上实现约 190 Hz 的 PID 平衡环、20 Hz 基于编码器的 PD 位置环（输出目标倾角），以及纯比例转向环。
  • 传感器融合：对板载 BMI270 IMU（400 Hz 采样）使用互补滤波（K = 0.98），开机自动标定陀螺仪零偏；车轮编码器采用中断计数。
  • 电机驱动：12 V 供电下的两路 DRV8871 驱动；编码器信号经分压保护 3.3 V 逻辑引脚；在整机上实测左右电机死区并分别补偿。
  • 非阻塞 BLE：自定义 BLE 服务每隔若干控制周期轮询一次，目标位置平滑过渡，避免指令干扰控制时序。
Engineering takeaway工程收获

After the midpoint demo the robot drifted and only balanced briefly. Rather than just retuning PID gains, the team fixed the root causes first — a rigid IMU mount, automatic calibration, measured motor deadband and a reinforced battery holder — then tuned the loops in order: balance → position → turning.

中期演示时机器人会漂移、只能短暂平衡。团队没有只调 PID 参数，而是先解决根本原因——固定 IMU、改为自动标定、实测电机死区、加固电池座——再按“平衡 → 位置 → 转向”的顺序逐级整定。

Jan 2025 – Apr 20252025.01 – 2025.04

Autonomous Coin-Picking Robot自主拾币机器人

ELEC 291 · Team of 66 人团队

A battery-powered robot that searches an area bounded by an AC perimeter wire, detects coins with a home-built metal detector and lifts them with a servo-driven electromagnet — by joystick, or fully on its own.

一台电池供电的机器人：在交流周界线围出的区域内搜索，用自制金属探测器发现硬币，再用舵机驱动的电磁铁拾取——可摇杆遥控，也可完全自主运行。

  • Two microcontroller families: an STM32L051 remote (joystick, LCD, buzzer) as master and a PIC32MX robot as slave, linked by JDY-40 serial radios; the remote converts joystick input to motor PWM values so the robot can focus on precise motor and servo timing.
  • Sensing circuits: a Colpitts-oscillator metal detector that registers coins by frequency shift, and inductor-plus-peak-detector boundary sensing of the AC perimeter, with thresholds set from oscilloscope and multimeter measurements.
  • Actuation: discrete NMOS/PMOS H-bridge for PWM motor drive; a two-servo arm with an electromagnet, moved slowly after pickup so coins aren't dropped.
  • Autonomy: a finite-state machine (forward → coin detected / perimeter → back off and turn) that alternates 45° and 90° turns to sweep the area and stops after 20 coins.
  • 两种微控制器：STM32L051 遥控端（摇杆、LCD、蜂鸣器）作为主机，PIC32MX 机器人作为从机，通过 JDY-40 串口无线通信；遥控端负责把摇杆输入换算为电机 PWM，让机器人端专注于精确的电机与舵机时序。
  • 检测电路：基于 Colpitts 振荡器、通过频率变化识别硬币的金属探测器；以电感加峰值检波的方式检测交流周界线；阈值均由示波器和万用表实测确定。
  • 执行机构：分立 NMOS/PMOS H 桥实现 PWM 电机驱动；双舵机机械臂配合电磁铁拾取，拾起后缓慢移动以防硬币掉落。
  • 自主逻辑：有限状态机（前进 → 检测到硬币 / 周界 → 后退并转向），交替 45° 与 90° 转向以覆盖整个区域，拾满 20 枚后停止。
Design decisions设计取舍

Sending PWM duty values instead of on/off direction bits gave fine speed control (and enabled a "slow push" mode). Making the remote the radio master avoided data collisions, and separate batteries for the detectors and the electromagnet cut electrical and magnetic noise.

传输 PWM 占空比而非简单的方向开关量，实现了精细调速（并支持“慢速推进”模式）；让遥控端担任无线主机避免了数据冲突；探测电路与电磁铁分开供电，降低了电气与磁场噪声。
